 Item 13, overstay:
  - Operators can set their own overstay price, within Parkly's limit.
  - Drivers are always charged at the price they booked at.
  - An overstay left unpaid for 24 hours blocks new bookings until it's paid.
  - Parkly staff can find a booking by code in the admin console and waive the overstay, with the reason recorded.
