**Parent Program:** Parkly GovTech (`parkly-app/governmentParking/`)  
**Status:** Ready to Implement  
**Dependencies:** SPRINT GOV-01, SPRINT GOV-02, SPRINT GOV-03, S7 (Operator Ledger & Settlement)

---

## 1. Objectives & Scope

Sprint GOV-05 builds the central transparency layer for the State Government leadership:

1. **The Executive Governor & Economic Council:** Requires high-level command-centre visibility into municipal revenue generation, urban mobility patterns, and cash leakage abatement across all Local Government Areas (LGAs).
2. **The State Internal Revenue Service (SIRS) & Auditor-General:** Requires microscopic, transaction-level accounting, bank statement auto-reconciliation, and tamper-proof audit trails for every kobo flowing into the Treasury Single Account (TSA).

### Core Deliverables

1. **Executive Governor Command Centre (`https://<stateSlug>.parkly.ng/executive`):**
   - **Real-Time IGR Revenue Counter:** Live ticker showing daily, weekly, and monthly municipal revenue collected.
   - **Leakage Abatement Metric:** Percentage comparison against historical baseline cash collections.
   - **City Congestion & Bay Utilization Heatmap:** Geospatial visualizer showing high-density occupancy corridors (e.g. Lagos Island, Victoria Island, Ikeja GRA).
   - **LGA Revenue Ranking:** Breakdown of parking revenue and violations across all local government areas.
2. **SIRS Treasury & Audit Portal (`https://<stateSlug>.parkly.ng/sirs`):**
   - **Daily TSA Remittance Journal:** Real-time feed of settled funds partitioned into State Consolidated Revenue, Parastatal Maintenance Quotas, and Technology Fees.
   - **Automated Bank Statement Reconciliation:** Auto-matching of Remita/Paystack settlement batches against bank settlement credits down to the exact kobo.
   - **Discrepancy Resolver:** Highlights unpaid gateway fees or pending settlement batches.
3. **Parastatal Maintenance Allocation Dashboard:**
   - Dedicated view for hospital chief medical directors, secretariat permanent secretaries, and chief registrars to track their facility's 15% maintenance allocation.
4. **Certified State Audit Exports:**
   - Cryptographically watermarked CSV and PDF monthly revenue statements conforming to Nigerian public sector accounting standards (IPSAS).

```typescript
    lga: string;

    occupancyPercent: number;
  }>;

```

### 2.2 Watermarked Public Audit Export Generator

All CSV and PDF exports generated for the Auditor-General or SIRS are dynamically stamped:

- Generated by: `Staff Name & Service ID`
- Cryptographic SHA-256 Hash of report content
- Official State Government Seal watermark
- Immutable audit log entry recorded in `audit_logs` table (`ACTION: GOV_AUDIT_REPORT_EXPORTED`)
