# Parkly Attendant Quick Field Guide (1-Page Laminated Reference)

> **Document:** On-Site Parking Attendant Reference Card  
> **Target Audience:** Parking lot security & barrier attendants using the Parkly Attendant PWA (`/attend`)  
> **Rule #1:** **NEVER COLLECT CASH FOR PARKING OR OVERSTAYS.** All transactions must go through the Parkly app to protect you and prevent audit discrepancies.

---

## 1. Scanner Status Colors & What To Do

| Screen Color | Sound / Haptic | Meaning | Attendant Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **🟢 GREEN** | Single High Beep | **VALID ENTRY / EXIT** | Lift barrier. Allow vehicle through immediately. |
| **🔵 BLUE** | Double Beep | **MONTHLY PASS (`PASS`)** | Allow through. Note: Only 1 vehicle per pass can be inside. |
| **🟡 YELLOW** | Short Alert | **EARLY ARRIVAL / EXTENDED** | Driver arrived within grace period or extended. Allow entry. |
| **🔴 RED** | Two Low Beeps | **REJECTED / UNPAID / INVALID** | **Do NOT lift barrier.** Check failure reason on screen. |

---

## 2. Common Red Screens & Solutions

### A. "PASS ALREADY CHECKED IN"
* **Why:** A monthly pass QR was scanned, but another car registered to that pass is already inside the car park.
* **Action:** Explain: *"Only one car can be parked on this pass at a time. The other vehicle must check out first, or you can book an hourly space on the app."*

### B. "OVERSTAY DUE" / "OUTSTANDING BALANCE"
* **Why:** The driver stayed longer than their booked window.
* **Action:**
  1. Do **NOT** accept cash!
  2. Ask driver to open their Parkly app and tap **"Pay Overstay"** on their active booking.
  3. Once the driver pays on their phone, tap **"Re-scan"** on your screen. It will turn **GREEN**.

### C. "BOOKING EXPIRED" / "NO ACTIVE BOOKING"
* **Why:** Driver booked for yesterday or a different time window.
* **Action:** Ask driver to open the Parkly app and create a fresh **"Park Now"** booking.

## 3. Plate & Code Lookup (When QR Camera Won't Scan)

If the driver's phone battery died, their screen is cracked, or the camera fails in direct sunlight:
1. Tap the **🔍 Search** tab at the top of your screen.
2. Type either:
   * The **License Plate Number** (e.g. `LND123AB`)
   * OR the **6-character Booking Code** (e.g. `PK-789012`)
3. Verify the vehicle details match the car in front of you.
4. Tap **Check In** or **Check Out**.

---

## 4. Offline Mode (No Cellular / Wi-Fi Network)

When the internet cuts out, the Parkly PWA automatically switches to **Offline Mode**:
* Look for the **"⚡ Offline Mode"** banner on top of the screen.
* You can continue scanning QR tickets normally! The device has the 24-hour manifest stored offline.
* All scans are queued safely on your phone.
* **Do NOT clear your browser history or log out.** Scans will sync automatically as soon as network returns.
