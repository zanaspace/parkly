Then, in Supabase:

1. Open your project (the qbhe… one) at supabase.com/dashboard.
2. In the left sidebar, click Authentication.
3. Under Configuration, click Attack Protection. Older dashboards have it under Project Settings → Authentication → Bot and Abuse Protection.
4. Turn on Enable Captcha protection.
5. Choose Captcha Provider: select Cloudflare Turnstile.
6. Captcha secret: paste the secret key (the one starting 0x4AAAAAAFsyON…), not the site key.
7. Click Save.
